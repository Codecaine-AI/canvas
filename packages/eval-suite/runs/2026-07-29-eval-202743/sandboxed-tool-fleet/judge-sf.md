Most consequential loss: The placement branch is materially miscommunicated: the reconstruction treats a quota/admission miss as the trigger for healthy-host selection and cold boot, whereas the brief requires over-quota requests to wait and reserves cold provisioning for a warm-pool miss.

Summary: The board communicates the domain, major components, lifecycle states, resource controls, reclamation outcome, quota guardrail, monitoring targets, and much of the request-to-destruction story. However, a system-defining placement branch is contradicted, so engineers could implement quota failure as cold-placement admission rather than fair waiting. Other important losses include the missing stopped-heartbeat trigger, absent warm-pool feedback inputs, no explicit removal of quarantined hosts from placement, incomplete tenant-capacity isolation, and no visible output route back to the agent. The result is useful for design discussion but not dependable as an implementation plan.
