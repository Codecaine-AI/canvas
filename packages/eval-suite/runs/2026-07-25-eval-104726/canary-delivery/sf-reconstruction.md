# SF blind system reconstruction

## SYSTEM PURPOSE
A 15-stage canary-delivery process with automatic rollback. It takes a signed source revision through protected-source controls, build and testing, security gates, immutable image publication with provenance, staging preparation and verification, production approval, a 5% primary-region canary, telemetry-based progressive rollout, audit and notification, and then a separately observed secondary-region canary with region-aware rollback.
