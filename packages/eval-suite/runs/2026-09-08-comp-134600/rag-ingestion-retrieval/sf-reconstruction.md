# SF blind system reconstruction

## SYSTEM PURPOSE
A retrieval-augmented knowledge system that ingests changing source material with ACLs, isolates ingestion failures, publishes only version-consistent indexes, performs permission-aware hybrid retrieval and grounded answering, collects feedback and coverage gaps, and rebuilds model generations in the background with atomic promotion and rollback.
