# SF blind system reconstruction

## SYSTEM PURPOSE
A support assistant that handles guarded live customer conversations, assembles replies from conversation history, durable customer facts and help-center articles, optionally performs billing/account actions, and escalates to human support when required. It distills durable memory after conversations, archives expired transcripts, tests versioned variants under automated judging and product-owner approval, and independently monitors live quality so regressing challengers can be removed or rolled back.
