# SF blind system reconstruction

## SYSTEM PURPOSE
Handle inbound calls by identifying and greeting the caller, obtaining recording and transcription consent, capturing intent through speech or keypad, routing eligible routine intents to AI self-service, transferring other or unresolved calls to human support with context and bounded waiting, and using call evidence to refine future AI eligibility.

- Query customer records by caller number: Looks up customer records using the caller number. [A process labeled "Query customer records by caller number" follows the inbound trunk.]
- Load account context / Personalized greeting: Loads matched account context and provides a personalized greeting. [The upper branch labeled "Match" enters a process labeled "Load account context / Personalized greeting".]
- Generic greeting: Provides a generic greeting when the customer-record query fails or finds no match. [The lower branch labeled "Failed / no match" enters the process "Generic greeting".]
- Capture recording & transcription consent: Captures the caller's consent concerning recording and transcription. [The personalized and generic greeting branches merge into a process labeled "Capture recording & transcription consent".]
- Short voice menu / Capture spoken intent: Presents a short voice menu and captures spoken intent. [A process labeled "Short voice menu / Capture spoken intent" follows consent capture.]
- Keypad intent entry: Captures intent through keypad entry when speech is noisy or unreliable. [A route labeled "Noisy / unreliable speech" leads from the voice-menu process to "Keypad intent entry".]
- AI-eligible routine intent?: Evaluates whether the captured intent is a routine intent eligible for AI handling. [Both the speech-understood route and the keypad route feed the process labeled "AI-eligible routine intent?" in region 2.]
- AI voice agent / Real-time conversation: Conducts a real-time voice conversation for AI-eligible routine intents. [The "Yes" route from "AI-eligible routine intent?" points to "AI voice agent / Real-time conversation".]

- Read-only account lookup service: Provides read-only account fact lookup. [A process labeled "Read-only account lookup service" is connected from the AI-agent area by a left-pointing arrow labeled "Retrieve facts".]
- Human queue / Transfer with context: Queues and transfers calls to human handling while carrying context. [A process labeled "Human queue / Transfer with context" receives the "No / person requested" route and the orange unresolved/person-requested/low-confidence route.]
- Human available within wait target?: Checks whether human support becomes available within the allowed waiting target. [A left-pointing arrow from the human queue enters the process "Human available within wait target?".]
- Human support / Continue with context: Continues the interaction with human support using transferred context. [The "Yes" route from the availability check points to "Human support / Continue with context".]
- Offer callback / End indefinite waiting: Offers a callback and stops indefinite waiting when the wait target is exceeded. [The downward branch labeled "No — wait target exceeded" enters "Offer callback / End indefinite waiting".]
- Handoff bundle: Carries the transcript, account context, detected intent, and work already completed so the caller need not repeat information. [A note titled "Handoff bundle" lists "Transcript," "Account context," "Detected intent," "Work already completed," and states "Caller need not repeat."]
- Record & transcribe calls / Quality evidence: Records and transcribes calls to produce quality evidence. [Region 4 contains a process labeled "Record & transcribe calls / Quality evidence".]
- Review unresolved & abandoned calls: Reviews calls classified as unresolved or abandoned. [A dashed purple route labeled "Unresolved / abandoned" leads to "Review unresolved & abandoned calls".]
- Adjust AI-eligible intents: Changes the scope of intents eligible for AI handling. [A dashed purple route labeled "Refine scope" leads from call review to "Adjust AI-eligible intents".]
